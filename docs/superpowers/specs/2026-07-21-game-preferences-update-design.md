# Game Preferences Update Design

## Goal

Update `Мои игровые предпочтения.md` with the user's latest positive and negative examples while preserving the existing progression-focused profile and avoiding rigid genre filters.

## Scope

This is a targeted editorial update, not a full rewrite. Keep the current headings, useful observations, linked reviews, and overall level of detail. Remove or reword text only where the new evidence makes it inaccurate or materially incomplete.

## Changes

- Clarify that the strongest positive loop is manual work becoming faster and eventually automated, often combined with a functional base, production, and selling output.
- Add content volume and sustained late-game variety as important criteria.
- Strengthen the need for visible goals without excluding open sandboxes; use No Man's Sky as the example of a compelling game weakened by aimlessness and an overloaded interface.
- Add a concise visual and UX standard: unattractive, derivative, cheap-looking presentation and obstructive interfaces are strong negatives.
- Do not reject all 2D games. Mindustry is a positive counterexample; the negative signal is cheap, unpleasant, or overly derivative execution.
- Do not reject all roguelikes. Generic roguelikes receive no genre bonus, while exceptionally polished or mechanically distinctive games such as Hades II and Balatro remain strong matches.
- Add Schedule I, Hydroneer, No Man's Sky, Hades II, Captain of Industry, Mindustry, and Ctrl Alt Ego as labeled positive evidence. Distinguish played-and-liked games from wishlist interest.
- Add the supplied rejected games as concise anti-examples grouped by reason instead of creating a long repetitive blacklist.
- Update the recommendation checklist so progression, direction, content depth, visual/UI quality, and originality are all checked explicitly.

## Style

Preserve the first-person Russian voice and practical recommendation purpose of the note. Use direct but neutral wording; retain the user's meaning without copying profanity into the durable profile. Avoid numeric scoring weights, a separate machine-readable schema, or claims that one disliked trait is an absolute veto.

## Verification

After editing, check that all new examples appear, Mindustry and Hades II prevent overbroad 2D/roguelike exclusions, played games are distinguished from wishlist games, and no existing core preference is contradicted.
